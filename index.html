What we do

At Carpe Delta we're passionate about helping businesses unlock the full potential of their data.

Customer data, sales data, inventory data all contribute to the story of your business, but connecting those pieces in a way that's useful can be a real challenge.  At Carpe Delta we turn messy, complex, and disconnected information into clear insights that support smarter decisions and meaningful progress. 

Too often valuable data goes to waste; trapped in spreadsheets, overlooked in silos, or lost in a sea of numbers. We help businesses get their data in order so they can save time, spot opportunities, make informed choices, and get the foundations right to take advantage of AI.

Every business is unique, so we work closely with you to understand your goals and challenges, then design and deliver tailored, data-centric solutions that make a real difference.

We're not just here to advise, we help make it happen.

Who we are

Carpe Delta Limited is a data consulting firm founded by Felicity Carpenter.

With over 20 years of experience across the aviation, energy, banking and transport sectors, Felicity brings deep expertise in analytics, data-driven strategy, digital transformation and marketing effectiveness. 

Her career spans senior consultant, management and implementation roles in both New Zealand and the UK and has a track record of demonstrating how data can enable confident, real-world decision-making.  She is passionate about simplifying processes, building capability and helping teams use data and AI well.

"I founded Carpe Delta because I believe data is one of the most valuable assets a business has if it's used well. I've seen what's possible with big budgets and skilled teams, and I believe smaller businesses deserve access to that same level of insight and impact.

Smart decisions don't have to be expensive; they just need the right expertise to get off the ground."

Why "Carpe Delta"?

"Carpe" means seize. "Delta" means change.

At Carpe Delta, we help you seize change, using your own data to drive it.
