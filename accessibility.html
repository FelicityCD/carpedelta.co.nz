Accessibility Statement

At Carpe Delta, we're committed to making our website accessible and inclusive for all visitors.

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, where practical, to ensure our content is clear, navigable, and usable by people of all abilities.

If you experience any difficulty accessing information on this site, or would like to request content in a different format, please get in touch via emailing felicity@carpedelta.co.nz.

We'll do our best to assist you as quickly as possible.
